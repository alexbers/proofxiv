## Role: weekly digest

Write the platform's weekly digest from `digest` in `materials.json` (new papers, papers verified this week, new open problems, confirmed literature events, moderation decisions).

Write Markdown for human readers (researchers and curious people), in English, at most ~600 words:
- a 2–3 sentence summary of the week;
- "Verified this week" — each paper with one line on what it proves (no hype, no claims beyond the materials);
- "Science news on catalog problems" — solved problems, improved records, prior art found;
- "New open problems in the catalog";
- "Moderation" — one line with counts.
Link papers as `/papers/<id>` and problems as `/problems/<slug>`. Do not invent anything that is not in the materials.
